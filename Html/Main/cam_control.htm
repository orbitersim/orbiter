Camera control

The Control tab in the camera dialog allows to tilt and move the camera, or rotate it around the target, depending on the current camera mode.

The button functions of this tab replicate keyboard shortcuts Ctrl ←, Ctrl →, Ctrl ↑, Ctrl ↓, Ctrl PgUp, Ctrl PgDn, mouse camera control (right button pressed and mouse wheel), and and joystick coolie hat operation.

In free ground observer mode, the speed slider allows to set the movement speed in m/s.

Camera field of view

The camera field of view (FoV) determines the aperture of the camera. A large field of view shows a large portion of the scene at small magnification, while a small field zooms in to a small area at large magnification.

Orbiter defines the field of view as the vertical aperture of the simulation window (the angle between the top and bottom edge of the window).

Use the slider to adjust the FoV in the range from 10° to 90°. To enter a precise value, Ctrl-click on the slider.

To change the field of view without opening the camera dialog, use Z to zoom in and X to zoom out. You can also use Ctrl Z and Ctrl X to zoom in and out in steps of 10°.

The current field of view (FoV) is shown in the information block in the top left corner of the simulation window. Note that Orbiter uses separate settings for internal (cockpit) and external views.
